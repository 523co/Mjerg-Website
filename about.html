About

What Mjerg is.

A moderation, levelling and utility bot for Osmium communities. 93 commands, live since July 2026, free with no paid tier.

It covers what a community usually ends up running three or four separate bots for: moderation with a record you can look up, levelling and an economy, auto-translation, games, and a stack of small utilities. There is no web dashboard. You set it up from chat, and !config shows every setting on one screen.

It is built and run by one person, Ruby, on a single server in New York. Worth knowing before you build a community around it: there is no company here and no support rota, just someone who runs Mjerg in their own community and notices when it breaks.

Nothing is behind a paywall and nothing is planned to be. Nothing from your community is sold, shared or used for training. The info page lists exactly what gets stored.

Mjerg is not an Osmium product. It is an independent bot built on Osmium's public API. Problems with the platform itself belong in Osmium's own channels.
