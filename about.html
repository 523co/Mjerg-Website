About

What Mjerg is.

I got tired of running four bots to keep one community working, so I wrote one that does the lot. That's Mjerg. 103 commands, up since July 2026, free.

It covers the things you'd usually end up with three or four separate bots for: moderation with a record you can actually look up, levelling and an economy, auto-translation, games, and a pile of small utilities. There's no web dashboard, and I'm not planning to write one. You set it up from chat, and !config puts every setting on one screen.

It's one person — me — and one server in New York. Worth knowing before you build a community around it: there's no company here and no support rota. I run Mjerg in my own community, so I usually notice when something breaks, but if it falls over while I'm asleep, it stays down until I'm not.

Nothing's behind a paywall and nothing is going to be. I don't sell anything from your community, don't share it, and don't train anything on it. The info page lists exactly what gets stored, line by line, and what doesn't.

Mjerg is not an Osmium product. It's an independent bot I built on Osmium's public API. If the platform itself is the problem, Osmium's own channels are the place to take it — I can't fix those.
