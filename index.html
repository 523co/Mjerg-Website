Auto-translation

Everyone reads the chat in their own language

Point Mjerg at a channel and it translates messages in and out of any language as people post. Someone writes in Portuguese, everyone else reads it in English, and the replies go back the other way. Nobody has to switch to English out of politeness.

  • Any language, both directions
  • Set per channel, not per community
  • Works on ordinary messages, no tagging
  • !translate for one-off lines

Levelling & economy

Levels, coins and a leaderboard

XP from talking, roles that unlock at a level you pick, and a leaderboard. Coins come from !work and get spent in a shop you stock yourself, or lost at !gamble. Both work out of the box and both can be switched off.

  • Level roles via !levelrole
  • Shop, balances and transfers
  • Wordle, hangman, trivia and counting

Add Mjerg in three steps

There's no invite link. Osmium handles it inside the app, and it takes about ten seconds.

  1. Open Osmium

    Desktop, web or mobile, all the same.

  2. Click on Mjerg

    Search for it, or click the name anywhere you see the bot posting.

  3. Hit "Add to Chat"

    Pick the community and confirm. You'll need Manage community there.

Ready to try it?

Mjerg is free, set up in about five minutes, and runs in the community it was built for.
